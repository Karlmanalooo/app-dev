# 🎥 My Favorite Movie: Fast & Furious

## 🚗 About the Movie

**Fast & Furious** is an American action film series created by *Rob Cohen* and produced by *Neal H. Moritz*.  
It follows **Dominic Toretto** and his crew as they take on dangerous missions involving street racing, heists, and global threats.  
The series is famous for its thrilling car chases, powerful vehicles, and the unbreakable bond of **family**.

> “I don’t have friends, I got family.” – Dominic Toretto

---

## 🏎️ Main Characters

1. **Dominic Toretto** – The fearless leader who values family above all else.  
2. **Brian O’Conner** – A former undercover cop who becomes Dom’s loyal friend.  
3. **Letty Ortiz** – A skilled street racer and Dom’s partner.  
4. **Roman Pearce** – The funny and confident member of the crew.  
5. **Tej Parker** – The tech expert who helps plan and execute missions.  

---

## 💥 My Favorite Moments

- 🚘 **Street Races** – High-speed competitions full of adrenaline.  
- 💣 **Heists** – Clever missions with intense car stunts and explosions.  
- 🌆 **Team Missions** – The crew works together to defeat powerful enemies.  
- ❤️ **Family Bonds** – The message that family always comes first.

## 🔥 Why I Love It

- Exciting car chases and nonstop action.  
- Strong theme of loyalty, trust, and teamwork.  
- Memorable quotes and emotional moments.  
- Characters who treat each other like real family.
